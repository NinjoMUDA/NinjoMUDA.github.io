Game Design

Unknown To Depth

Game concept & design doc · Team Game4Life (5) · 2023 · 37 slides

A third-person roguelite with a time loop. A cursed hero respawns in "the Depth" every time he dies, and each death reshapes the floor through a butterfly effect. To break the curse he has to climb 10 floors of a tower, each one its own world.

  • 10 themed floors with a boss mechanic each: a World Tree that revives the boss until you destroy it, an underwater kingdom where you hunt for air rooms, a three-headed elemental dragon, a library outside time with debris falling from rifts
  • Core loop and economy: Souls (permanent stat upgrades, hired troops, dealer) vs Coins (shop items for the current run), plus artifacts, cores (weapon evolution) and runes (perks)
  • Room system: players read the symbols on the doors to choose their path: shop, perk, soul (more risk, more reward), casino, puzzle, chest
  • Meta-progression: perk rooms you clear stay unlocked from floor 1 on the next run, and perk slots grow from 1 to 4
  • Iteration: later reworked into a 2.5D action-RPG with slash, dash, parry and spells, and stat requirements that soften instead of locking gear

Feywood

Playable prototype · 2D action survival · Unity / C# · team of 4 · Nov – Dec 2024

Our team's first game, a playable prototype: simple controls, auto-attacking weapons and mounting chaos. Its identity is "a blend of whimsy and intensity".

  • Main menu, character select, weapons that level up (damage, extra projectiles, area scaling), 9 passive items (Cloak, Bracer, Comet, Lantern…), XP gems with drop rates, and an endless chunk-based map
  • I implemented the XP-gem magnet pickup in C# (pull radius scales with the player's Magnet stat), and researched game-design techniques, color schemes, and character and item design

Post-mortem: it plays from start to finish but was never balanced, and it still has known bugs. The next step would be tuning the waves and enemy stats.
