Dungeon Drift

In developmentRoguelike × auto-battler Unity 6 · HD-2DJun 2026 – now

You play a god who isn't at full power yet. You send a human party into the dungeon, arrange them before each fight, and then they battle on their own. Faith you bring back makes your god stronger for the next run.

My part: I lead the design and balance: the world pillars, more than 50 numbered design decisions, and every tuning pass. The code is written with AI coding agents (Claude, Codex) that I direct and review. Most of the art is by my teammate Arth.

  • Theme that justifies the mechanicAuto-battle is usually a limitation. Here the player is a god, so a god doesn't fight; it plans, blesses and chooses the path. The constraint becomes the fantasy.
  • Balance by measurementI tune with recorded team fights, not gut feel: Bow range 6.5 → 8 and Rifle 7 → 9 until Rifle ≈ Bow, Mace stun 20% → 30%. I also flagged Greatsword T4 as an outlier (49 DPS vs 22–38).
  • Cutting what didn't workI tried Tug-of-War lanes and a TFT-style grid, cut both, and moved to free-form clashes where formation still matters: the front line walls off the back row.
  • Reward loopEvery fight ends in a pick screen (gold, rival loot, boss artifact 1-of-3), then a skill pick and a level-up summary, before you return to the map.

Feywood

Playable prototype2D action survival Unity · C#Team of 4Nov–Dec 2024

Our team's first game: simple controls, auto-attacking weapons and mounting chaos. The identity we aimed for was "a blend of whimsy and intensity."

My part: I implemented the XP-gem magnet pickup in C# (its pull radius scales with the player's Magnet stat), and researched game-design techniques, color schemes, and character and item design.

  • What's in itMain menu, character select, weapons that level up (damage, extra projectiles, area), 9 passive items, XP gems with drop rates, and an endless chunk-based map.

Post-mortem: it plays from start to finish but was never balanced, and it still has known bugs. The next step would be tuning the waves and enemy stats.

Unknown To Depth

Concept & design docRoguelite · time loop Team Game4Life (5)37 slides2023

A cursed hero respawns in the Depth every time he dies, and each death reshapes the floor through a butterfly effect. To break the curse he has to climb a tower of 10 worlds.

  • Two-currency economySouls buy permanent upgrades and hired troops; Coins buy shop items for the current run. Artifacts, cores and runes add stat boosts, weapon evolution and perks.
  • Readable room choicesA symbol on each door shows what's behind it (shop, perk, soul room, casino, puzzle or chest), so every path is a risk/reward decision.
  • Meta-progressionPerk rooms you clear stay unlocked from floor 1 on the next run, and perk slots grow from 1 to 4.
  • IterationLater reworked into a 2.5D action-RPG with slash, dash, parry and spells, where under-levelled gear gets weaker instead of locked.
